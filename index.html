Privacy Policy

Application: Earth Explorer - Flight Simulator
Effective date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nabil Ansari ("Service Provider", "we", "us", or "our") handles information in the Earth Explorer - Flight Simulator mobile application (the "Application"). The Application is supported by advertising and may offer optional in-app purchases.

Information the Application and its services process

Depending on the platform, features enabled, and your choices, the Application and the third-party services it uses may process:

  • Device and technical information: IP address, device model, operating system, app version, language, network status, advertising identifier, approximate location inferred from an IP address, diagnostics, and interaction or usage information.
  • Advertising information: ad requests, impressions, interactions, reward-ad completion, fraud-prevention signals, and information used to show, measure, limit, or personalize advertising. Google Mobile Ads and Unity Ads may process this information under their own policies.
  • Google Play Games information: if you sign in, Google may process your Play Games account identifier, public player name, achievements, leaderboard scores, and authentication status. The Application uses this information to provide sign-in, achievements, and leaderboards.
  • Purchase information: Google Play or another applicable app store processes purchase, entitlement, restore, and transaction information for the optional "Remove Ads" purchase. We do not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Network and security checks: the Application may make connectivity requests to Google and Unity advertising endpoints and locally inspect network-interface information to identify unavailable ad services, VPN-like interfaces, or ad-blocking configurations. This is used to protect rewarded features and does not collect the contents of your network traffic.

Location information

The Application does not request or collect precise GPS location from your device. Flight locations shown in the Application are game or map data. Third parties may infer an approximate location from an IP address when delivering ads or online content.

Analytics and local storage

Unity Analytics may process app events, diagnostics, device information, and usage information where it is enabled in the released build. The Application also stores limited preferences locally on your device, such as settings and whether an ad-removal entitlement has been observed. Local preferences are not a user account and are not used to store payment-card information.

Tileset and map data services

The Application uses Cesium for Unity to process and render tileset data. The current scene is configured to request Cesium World Terrain from Cesium ion (ion asset ID 1) and may request raster imagery from the configured EOX tile service. These services may receive your IP address, device and request information needed to deliver the tileset or imagery. Cesium for Unity is also used as the in-app data-processing and rendering library.
